Nature GP

Independent advisory on nature-related risk, biodiversity measurement, and evidence synthesis.

The natural world is changing rapidly, with unpredictable knock-on economic and geopolitical effects. Institutional interest in measuring and managing impacts and dependencies on nature has often run ahead of what current data and metrics can provide.

Navigating that gap takes breadth—across natural and social sciences, geographies, and regulatory and financial framing—and a healthy scepticism about metrics, earned by having generated some of the underlying field data. Nature GP is where I put that to work.

Typical engagements

  • Independent evaluation and reviewof metrics, methods, and evidence
  • Measurement framework designfor biodiversity and nature-related outcomes
  • Nature-risk evaluation and stress-testingfor corporates, financial institutions, and standard-setters

About

Nature GP is run by Dr Graham Prescott: field ecologist turned nature-finance specialist. PhD in tropical landscape ecology (Cambridge), Lead Author on the IPBES Nexus Assessment, and formerly Technical Director of Science at The Biodiversity Consultancy, where he wrote the TNFD guidance on measuring changes to the state of nature and led development of the measurement framework of the Verra Nature Framework.
